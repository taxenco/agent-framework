# Clarify before building

Do not plan or delegate on a guess.

## Steps

1. List what is unclear: goal, scope, constraints, success criteria.
2. Ask one question at a time. Prefer multiple choice; put your recommendation first.
3. Skip anything you can learn from the codebase instead of asking.
4. Play back your understanding in a few lines and ask for correction.
5. Stop once you are confident enough to write a plan. Do not over-interview.

## Output

- Small change: agree in chat and proceed.
- Larger change: a short written spec (goal, scope, out of scope, acceptance criteria) handed to the `planner`.
